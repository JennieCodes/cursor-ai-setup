## Post 1

**Topic:** Founder Story / Personal Brand

### Summary
Justin shares his decision to leave a $550,000 corporate salary after burnout and explains how that choice led him to build a business that gave him purpose, income, and time freedom. He frames the post around the idea that personal timing is not the problem and that meaningful work can compound over time.

### LinkedIn Content Insight
The post uses a personal transformation story to build trust and authority before introducing a lead magnet. It follows a common founder-led content pattern: hook, story, lesson, and CTA.

### Key Takeaway
Founder storytelling is effective on LinkedIn because it creates emotional connection and positions the creator as credible before making an offer.
