## Post 1

**Date:** June 12, 2026

**Source URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/erinbalsa_a-lot-of-b2b-tech-companies-get-to-10-30m-activity-7471157607622316032-Gfbv

**Topic:** Using Original Research to Scale Beyond Founder-Led Growth

### Summary

Erin explains that many B2B technology companies initially reach $10M–$30M ARR through founder relationships, referrals, and existing networks. However, as the company grows, the founder’s reputation alone becomes insufficient for continued expansion. Original research can help bridge this reputation gap by providing evidence-based insights that establish the company itself as a trusted authority in its category.

### LinkedIn Content Insight

Content is not always designed to generate immediate leads. Strategic content assets such as original research reports can build brand credibility, create wider market recognition, and help companies earn the attention of senior decision-makers.

### Key Takeaway

As B2B SaaS companies mature, they must transition from relying on founder credibility to building institutional authority. High-quality original research can become a powerful trust-building asset.

## Post 2

**Date:** June 16, 2026

**Source URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/erinbalsa_how-to-publish-a-data-backed-research-report-activity-7472600113585238016-q_SK

**Topic:** Why Research Credibility Matters More Than a “Data-Backed” Label

### Summary

Erin criticizes the practice of presenting small, low-quality polls as formal research reports. She argues that valuable research requires transparency around methodology, sample size, and data sources. Buyers are increasingly skeptical and can identify when brands use statistics without meaningful evidence.

### LinkedIn Content Insight

The effectiveness of research-driven content depends on trust. Transparent methodologies, honest reporting, and accurate representation of data make thought leadership more credible and more likely to influence buyers.

### Key Takeaway

Strong B2B thought leadership does not come from simply attaching statistics to content. It comes from producing research that is rigorous, transparent, and genuinely useful to the audience.
