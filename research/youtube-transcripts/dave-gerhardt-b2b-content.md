# Dave Gerhardt

## Video Details

**Title:** Content, Community, and Building a 200,000+ B2B Audience  
**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S3jOSiE6l80

---

## Video Summary

Dave Gerhardt discusses how modern B2B brands combine content, community, and distribution to create long-term growth. The discussion covers the role of LinkedIn, podcasts, SEO, and owned communities in building awareness and generating demand.

---

## Key Content Strategy Insights

- Content should be distributed across multiple channels.
- LinkedIn and podcasts help build long-term audience trust.
- Communities create stronger engagement and brand loyalty.
- Consistent educational content can become a demand generation engine.

---

## Relevance to B2B SaaS LinkedIn Strategy

The video shows how B2B SaaS marketers can use content ecosystems, audience ownership, and community-building to strengthen their LinkedIn organic growth strategy.
