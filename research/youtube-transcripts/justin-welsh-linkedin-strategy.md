# Justin Welsh

## Video Details

**Title:** How to Grow and Monetize Your LinkedIn Audience  
**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D_12KNeS07k

---

## Video Summary

Justin Welsh explains how creators and professionals can use LinkedIn to build an audience that converts into business opportunities. He emphasizes choosing a specific niche, creating useful content consistently, and attracting the right audience instead of focusing only on follower numbers. He also discusses using LinkedIn as a platform to establish authority and generate leads.

---

## Key Content Strategy Insights

- A clear niche attracts a more relevant audience.
- Valuable educational content builds authority.
- Audience quality matters more than follower count.
- LinkedIn content should support a business goal such as lead generation or product sales.

---

## Relevance to B2B SaaS LinkedIn Strategy

This video demonstrates key principles that B2B SaaS companies can apply: defining a target audience, creating expertise-driven content, and using LinkedIn as an organic acquisition channel.
