# Research Methodology

## Purpose

This repository was created to support a future B2B SaaS LinkedIn organic growth playbook. The research focuses on public content from practitioners and creators who publish about LinkedIn strategy, B2B content, demand generation, audience research, SEO, AI search, and thought leadership.

## Expert Selection Criteria

Experts were selected based on:

- Relevance to B2B SaaS organic growth or content strategy
- Active public publishing on LinkedIn, YouTube, or adjacent channels
- Evidence of practitioner experience or repeated strategic commentary
- Usefulness for developing practical LinkedIn playbook principles
- Diversity across founder-led content, demand generation, SEO, audience research, original research, and content operations

## Collection Approach

The research combined manual review of public sources with AI-assisted workflows for organization, summarization, and synthesis. LinkedIn posts and YouTube videos were prioritized when they directly supported the assignment goal.

The repository preserves:

- A source list of 10 selected experts
- Deep LinkedIn analysis for the highest-priority experts
- YouTube transcript notes for selected priority topics
- A cross-expert synthesis of recurring strategic patterns

## Scope Boundaries

This is a curated qualitative research project, not a complete quantitative dataset. The repository does not claim to include every recent post, complete engagement metrics, full transcript exports, or statistical performance analysis.

Some experts were retained as additional sources for future expansion because their work is relevant to the playbook, even though deep analysis files were not created for every expert in the initial research pass.

## Use of AI Assistance

AI-assisted workflows were used to help organize notes, summarize collected public content, and identify recurring strategic patterns. Human review guided expert selection, relevance judgments, source organization, and final synthesis.

## Limitations

- Public content availability varies by platform.
- LinkedIn access and visibility can vary by account, region, and login state.
- The analysis emphasizes strategic usefulness over exhaustive coverage.
- The current repository is a foundation for a future playbook, not the final playbook itself.
